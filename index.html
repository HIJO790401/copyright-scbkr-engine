Engine Overview / 引擎說明

核心說明 1 / Core Statement 1

這不是單純授權平台，而是授權裁決引擎。

This is not a simple licensing platform, but a licensing decision engine.

核心說明 2 / Core Statement 2

它不是只問「能不能用」，而是問：誰在申請、為什麼使用、在哪個邊界內成立、何時失效、失效後誰負責。

It does not only ask “can this be used?” It asks who is applying, why it is used, under which boundary it is valid, when it fails, and who bears responsibility after failure.

核心說明 3 / Core Statement 3

Semantic Responsibility Hash 不是隨機碼，而是一次授權事件在特定邊界與責任鏈下的公式簽章。

A Semantic Responsibility Hash is not a random string, but a formula-based signature of a licensing event under a specific boundary and responsibility chain.

      SCBKR Explanation / SCBKR 說明區

      在結果區與說明區一致顯示 SCBKR 五維
      Show the same five SCBKR dimensions in both the result area and explanation area

      S — Subject / 主體

      誰申請、誰代表單位、誰作為責任主體進場。

      Who applies, who represents the organization, and who enters as the accountable subject.

      C — Cause / 因果

      為何使用、用途鏈條為何、來源內容如何收束到目標應用。

      Why it is used, what the usage chain is, and how the source content is narrowed into the target application.

      B — Boundary / 邊界

      法律邊界、技術邊界、生成邊界、再分發邊界、商用邊界。

      Legal, technical, generative, redistribution, and commercial boundaries.

      K — Cost Cluster / 成本簇

      內容生產成本、編輯人力、資料價值、替代風險、現實代價、資源消耗。

      Content production cost, editorial labor, data value, substitution risk, real-world cost, and resource consumption.

      R — Responsibility / 責任

      誰承擔爭議、誰接回報、誰負責追蹤、誰對越界使用負責。

      Who bears disputes, who receives reports, who is responsible for tracing, and who is responsible for out-of-boundary use.

      Boundary Models / 邊界模型區

      固定展示 7 種邊界模型與簡短說明
      Display the seven fixed boundary models with short explanations

      READ_ONLY / 僅閱讀

      僅允許人工閱讀與參考，不得納入生成或訓練流程。

      Only allows human reading and reference, without entering generation or training workflows.

      INDEX_ONLY / 僅索引

      可建立檢索索引，但不得用於內容重寫或生成替代。

      May be indexed for retrieval, but not used for rewriting or substitute generation.

      SUMMARY_ONLY / 僅摘要

      內容可被濃縮說明，但不得形成可替代原文市場的輸出。

      Content may be condensed into summaries, but must not create outputs that substitute the original market.

      RAG_ASSIST / 檢索輔助

      可作為檢索輔助依據，但不得把來源內容直接吸收成模型權重。

      May support retrieval-augmented responses, but must not be absorbed into model weights.

      TRAINING_USE / 訓練使用

      內容被納入模型學習或微調流程，風險與責任顯著提高。

      Content is incorporated into model learning or fine-tuning, increasing both risk and responsibility.

      COMMERCIAL_GEN / 商業生成

      內容被用於直接或間接商業輸出，需重新審視授權與責任邊界。

      Content is used for direct or indirect commercial generation, requiring renewed licensing and responsibility review.

      REDISTRIBUTION / 再分發

      內容或其衍生結果被再次傳播或提供給第三方。

      Content or its derivatives are redistributed or delivered to third parties.

      Result States / 判決狀態區

      固定展示 5 種判決狀態與法務含義
      Display the five fixed decision states and their governance meaning

      ALLOW / 允許

      在授權邊界內成立。

      Valid within the declared licensing boundary.

      WARN / 警示

      暫時成立，但需附加限制、回報或人工複核。

      Conditionally valid, but requires added restrictions, reporting, or human review.

      NON-CLOSABLE / 不可閉合

      責任鏈無法閉合，必須人工介入。

      The responsibility chain cannot be closed and requires human intervention.

      VOID / 歸零

      該使用事件不具成立資格。

      The usage event does not qualify as valid.

      OVERRUN / 越界

      原授權可能成立，但當前使用已超出原始邊界。

      The original license may be valid, but the current use has exceeded its boundary.
